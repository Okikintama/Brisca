THIS GAME USES FAKE MONEY ONLY

Every player starts with $200.00 of pretend money. Real money can never be added. There are no purchases and no payment screens. Nothing here can be cashed out.

REAL GAMBLING RISKS

  • Losing money: the odds are built so the house wins over time.
  • Chasing losses: betting more to win back what you lost usually makes losses bigger.
  • Addiction: gambling can become a compulsion that is hard to stop.
  • Family and work: it can lead to debt, lies, fights, missed work and broken trust.

WARNING SIGNS

  • Betting more than you planned, or more than you can afford to lose.
  • Needing bigger bets to feel the same excitement.
  • Hiding gambling or lying about how much you spend.
  • Borrowing money or selling things to gamble.
  • Feeling restless or irritable when trying to cut back.
  • Gambling to escape stress or bad feelings.

HOW TO SET LIMITS

  • Set a money limit before you start, and stop when you reach it.
  • Set a time limit and take breaks.
  • Never chase losses. Never borrow to gamble.
  • Don't gamble when upset, stressed or drinking.
  • Use self-exclusion tools and tell someone you trust.

NEED HELP?

Call 1-800-GAMBLER (US) · free, confidential, 24/7.
